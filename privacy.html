Privacy

Your information, handled with care.

This notice explains what The Numbers Lab collects through this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you contact us through the website, we may receive your name, company, email address, phone number and the information you include in your message.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, understand the support you may need and continue a business conversation you have requested. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and sharing

Website enquiries may be delivered through our hosting and email providers. We limit access to people and service providers who need the information to operate the website or respond to you.

Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the conversation, our business records or applicable legal requirements.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction or ask us to delete information where appropriate.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, email Hello@thenumberslab.co.za.
